🚲 PedalMath

Your power meter reports a number; your body cashes it. PedalMath converts watts into the things you actually care about: an honest FTP, zones you can train in, how fast those watts move you on the flat and up a climb, and what the effort cost in food.

  • FTP from your test - 95% of a 20-minute all-out, or 75% of your best ramp minute. No ego adjustment either way.
  • Seven training zones - computed from your FTP, from active recovery to sprint.
  • Watts to speed - real physics (aero drag, rolling resistance, gravity on the gradient), solved numerically. 200W on the flat is about 34 km/h; on a 5% climb it is about 15.
  • Climb time - pick a gradient and a length, get the honest clock. 250W up an Alpe d'Huez profile is just over an hour, and the math does not care how it looks on video.
  • The 1:1 food rule - at roughly 24% efficiency, a kJ at the pedals is a food kcal. Your ride snack, accounted.
